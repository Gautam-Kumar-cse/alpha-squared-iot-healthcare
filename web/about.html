About Alpha Squared IoT Healthcare

Alpha Squared IoT Healthcare aims to improve patient safety through IoT-based health monitoring, emergency assistance, location tracking, and health analytics. We bridge clinical-grade wearable sensors with instant cloud intelligence to protect vulnerable individuals, post-operative patients, and lone workers.

INTELLIGENT HEALTHCARE ECOSYSTEM

Proactive Care, Autonomous Alerts, & Sub-Second Telemetry

Traditional patient care often relies on periodic manual spot-checks or delayed emergency calls. Alpha Squared reimagines emergency response by uniting physical wearable micro-controller nodes (ESP32) with real-time biometric analysis and autonomous emergency escalation. When critical vital anomalies, abrupt fall impacts, or distress signals occur, the system dispatches coordinates and alert notifications in under two seconds.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Comprehensive Patient Safety Ecosystem

Continuous biometrics telemetry combined with autonomous incident detection algorithms.

Real-Time Vitals Tracking

Continuous pulse rate, blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), and core body temperature monitoring with sub-second cloud synchronization.

Autonomous Fall Detection

Integrated 6-axis IMU algorithmic analysis identifies abrupt impact and posture change, initiating a timed escalation workflow.

Immediate Caregiver SOS

Physical emergency button on the wearable hardware paired with deliberate two-step software triggers for rapid incident broadcasting.

Live Geolocation Tracking

Precision device GPS coordinate logging with seamless browser fallback and one-tap Google Maps directions for emergency responders.

Longitudinal Health Analytics

Historical trends analysis with interactive Chart.js visualizations, configurable timeline zoom, and anomaly flags.

Role-Based Security Rules

Strict Firebase authorization policies ensure only authorized patients, primary physicians, and caregivers can access sensitive vitals.
