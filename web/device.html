Hardware Team Pinout & Driver Abstraction Contract

Because specific sensor part numbers are finalized during physical assembly, the firmware implements a clean Hardware Abstraction Layer (HAL). The firmware in hardware/esp32/ automatically binds to any compatible sensor using standardized configuration pins.

Subsystem Supported Hardware Options Standard ESP32 GPIO Pins Status
Pulse & SpO2 MAX30102 / MAX30100 Optical Sensor GPIO 21 (SDA), GPIO 22 (SCL) DRIVER READY
Temperature DS18B20 Digital / NTC Thermistor GPIO 4 (OneWire) or GPIO 34 (ADC) DRIVER READY
Fall Detector MPU6050 / ADXL345 6-Axis IMU GPIO 21 (SDA), GPIO 22 (SCL) DRIVER READY
GPS Positioning u-blox NEO-6M / NEO-8M UART Module GPIO 16 (RX2), GPIO 17 (TX2) DRIVER READY
